House rules

About this site

About SmokeRoach

SmokeRoach (sometimes written Smoke Roach) is a budget travel YouTube channel from Liverpool, UK. Scott, Charlotte and their dog Gunner do travel price challenges: cheap flights, meal deals, all-inclusives on a shoestring and honest reviews of every penny. We also make free browser games, the SmokeRoach games, write budget travel guides from our own trips, sell official SmokeRoach merch, and run the SmokeRoach Club, the Low Cost Legends Club.

This is the official SmokeRoach website: smokeroach.github.io. Our only official accounts are YouTube @smoke_roach, Instagram @smoke_roach, TikTok @smoke_roach and X @blendyfella.
