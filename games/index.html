SmokeRoach Game: play free online

Welcome to the home of the SmokeRoach game. SmokeRoach is a budget travel channel from Liverpool, made by Scott, Charlotte and their dog Gunner. We turn our real price challenges into little games you can play for free, straight in your browser. There is nothing to download and no sign-up, and every game works on your phone as well as a computer.

There are three SmokeRoach games so far. Start with the £99 Challenge if you want a quick go, try Scooter Surfers for a 3D run across Europe, or jump into Amsterdam Open World to explore a whole city with your mates.

How the SmokeRoach games started

Our videos are price challenges: can you do an Amsterdam trip from the UK for £99, or a week of all-inclusive winter sun for £249? The games take the same idea and make it something you can play on the bus. The £99 Challenge is the budget run, Scooter Surfers is the trip across Europe, and Amsterdam Open World is the city itself, street by street.

Want to see the real trips? Watch them on YouTube at @smoke_roach, or read our budget travel guides. Back to the SmokeRoach home page.

SmokeRoach game FAQ

Are the SmokeRoach games free?

Yes. All three SmokeRoach games are free to play in your web browser. There is nothing to download and no sign-up.

Do the SmokeRoach games work on phones?

Yes. They run in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone you tap and swipe; on a computer you can use the keyboard.

Who is in the SmokeRoach game?

Scott, Charlotte and Gunner, the crew behind the SmokeRoach budget travel channel on YouTube.

Can I play the SmokeRoach game with friends?

Yes, in SmokeRoach: Amsterdam Open World. Press Play Online to explore with up to 8 players, with voice chat.

Where can I watch the real trips?

On the SmokeRoach YouTube channel, @smoke_roach. The games are based on our budget travel price challenges from Liverpool.
