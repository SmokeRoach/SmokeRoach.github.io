HOW TO PLAY

On foot

W A S D move · Space jump · Left click / J PUNCH (tap = 10 damage · while moving = quick 5 · hold ~1 s = 50 haymaker)
Mouse look (click to lock) · E / F USE: ride the nearest bike, scooter, car or boat, climb ladders, flip the red light switch, start missions

Guns (backstreet dealer)

Find the red AMMO sign in an alley (red dot on the map) and press E. Handgun £150 · 10 dmg · 48 rds (12/mag) · SMG £400 · 2 dmg rapid · 180 rds (30/mag) · Sniper £800 · 50 dmg · 15 rds (5/mag) with scope. Ammo refill = 25% of the gun price. 1-in-20 chance of a free GOLD MINIGUN (unlimited ammo).
Right mouse / K aim (first person) · Left click / J fire · R reload · 1-5 / G switch weapon (1 = fists). Touch: AIM + FIRE buttons, 🔫 to switch. Cartoon hits only — no shooting in the coffeeshops.

The Gunner's Den

Three floors of coffeeshop by the canal. Talk to the budtender at the till on the ground floor (E) to buy strains — collect all 8 (🌿 x/8 on the HUD). Each one has its own silly 10–15 s screen effect, and any purchase has a 1 in 8 chance to make you hit white in the Dam. Sit on a seat upstairs (E) for the lounge chat room. Collect all 8 to unlock the final level…

Red-light windows

Walk up to a window and press E to say hello. Tip £5 and she'll step out for a ridiculous glitch dance or a cheeky dancehall routine with you — or she might just leg it with your fiver, so chase her down!

Driving

W/S throttle & brake/reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake · H bell/horn · E get off

Other

M map · Tab players · T chat · C camera reset · Esc/P pause

420 jet pack

Get on and off a scooter 4 times and the 420 jet pack drops in front of you. Walk into it or press E. Space thrust up · Shift/Ctrl down · W A S D fly. Land on any rooftop and walk around up there.

Online & voice

PLAY ONLINE, pick a nickname, join the public lobby or a room code (max 8). Press 🎤 to allow your mic, then hold V to talk or toggle open mic. Voices are positional — players closer to you sound louder. Tab players list & mute.

Goals

Find Gunner, do the Bitterballen Run, race the canal and the Wallen Sprint, pick up gold coins for spending money, and collect all 10 travel stamps for your passport. The playable area is the De Wallen core — the yellow dashed line on the map. Ride recklessly and the Politie will come for you — lose them to drop your heat.

Touch: left stick to move, drag right side to look, buttons for jump / use / punch / bell. Online: hold the big TALK button to speak.
