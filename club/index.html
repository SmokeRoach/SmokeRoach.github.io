SmokeRoach Club: the Low Cost Legends Club

The SmokeRoach Club is the Low Cost Legends Club (LCLC), the budget travel community from SmokeRoach. We're Scott and Charlotte (plus Gunner the dog) from Liverpool, and we've trial-and-errored loads of trips to find the best possible price. The club is where we share the money savers we use ourselves: real receipts from our videos, tested on our own trips, written for UK travellers.

💷 Visit the Low Cost Legends Club →

What's in the club

  • Free for everyone: starter guides with no sign-up, like the SmokeRoach Starter (10 money savers we use on every trip), beating bag fees on Ryanair, easyJet and Jet2, spending money abroad without getting stung, and the UK traveller's checklist for Europe.
  • Membership from £4.99 a month: full Price Challenge write-ups, cheapest places to go from the UK, and where-to-go guides from our trips. A higher tier adds our hidden gems and coupon codes. Cancel any time.
  • For UK travellers: everything is tested for getting the best price from UK airports, in pounds. Prices are what we paid or found when we checked, and they change, so always check before you book.

What is the SmokeRoach Club?
