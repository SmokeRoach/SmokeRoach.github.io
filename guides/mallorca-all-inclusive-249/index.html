Price Challenge #2 · Mallorca

Mallorca all-inclusive for £249

Watch the full video on YouTube: £249 ALL-INCLUSIVE MALLORCA ☀️ Winter Sun Deal | Price Challenge #2

Do £249 all-inclusive holidays still exist? In October we (Scott and Charlotte) went to Cala Ratjada in Mallorca for a week of winter sun, and the package cost £249 each with flights, hotel, food and drink included. This was Price Challenge #2. Here is exactly what we paid and what you get.

What we actually spent (2 people, 1 week, October)

  • ✈️ Holiday package: £249 each. Ryanair from Liverpool Airport, booked through loveholidays.com.
  • 🏨 Hotel: Club 07 Cala Ratjada, all-inclusive.
  • 🛵 Scooter hire: a 125cc for the week, £80.
  • 🍴 Extras: about £100 on restaurants and a boat tour.
  • 🧾 Total: about £680 for the two of us, so roughly £340 each.

What "all-inclusive" actually got us

Because it was only £249, we didn't expect much. It was better than we thought.

  • Drinks on tap: proper Coke and Fanta, beer, red, white and rosé wine, water, and every juice you want (apple, orange and more). We also had loads of mocktails and lots of tea.
  • The buffet: cheeses, salads, a few different pastas with sauce you add yourself, lasagna, meatballs in tomato sauce, stuffed peppers, rice, fish, chips, then soft cakes, fruit and ice cream at the end.
  • Snacks all day: burgers and hot dogs cooking outside, and food late at night too. We turned up at 1am on the first night and there was still a late dinner on.
  • Things to do: pools, tennis courts, a games room with pool tables and arcade machines (most were 50 cents or a euro), ping pong on certain days, and loads of entertainment in the hotel.

Getting around: hire a scooter

For £80 the 125cc scooter gave us the whole island for the week. One night we rode it from one side of the island to the other, and we took it off-road up into the mountains too. One tip: it gets cold in bits on a scooter at night, even in October, so pack a warm layer. One of us had three coats on.

Cala Ratjada or Magaluf?

Cala Ratjada is on the other side of the island from Magaluf. Some people call it the posh bit. We found it cleaner and nicer than the Magaluf side, and it's definitely worth it.

Want the same deal?

We got ours through loveholidays.com. DM us on Instagram and we'll send you the voucher code we used. Prices are what we paid in October 2026 and they change every day, so treat them as a guide, not a promise.
