How to · Coach + ferry

Amsterdam to Liverpool for £42

Watch the full video on YouTube: AMSTERDAM to LIVERPOOL for £42 🚌 Coach + Ferry | How To

This is the cheapest way Scott found to get home from Amsterdam to Liverpool: £42 by coach and ferry. It's a long day, but it's simple. Here's how it works, step by step.

1. Find the FlixBus at Amsterdam Centraal

Walk through Amsterdam Centraal station and keep going right through to the back. That's where the coaches leave from. Look for the big FlixBus.

2. Passport control at Calais

The coach takes you through customs at Calais. It was quick and easy: about 5 minutes. If you've got a backpack, you put your one bag through the check, then get back on the coach.

3. The ferry across

The coach goes onto the ferry, and you get off and walk round while you cross. There are a few decks to explore, so stretch your legs.

4. Change at London Victoria

Coaches from the continent stop at a London coach station. Scott's stop was London Victoria Coach Station, where he had a layover and went for a walk round London before the last leg.

5. The last leg to Liverpool

From Victoria it's another FlixBus up north. It takes about four to five hours to Liverpool, with one more pick-up at Finchley Road about half an hour after you leave Victoria.

Our tips

  • Pack light. One backpack keeps the scanner at Calais quick.
  • Use the London layover to stretch your legs. Scott went for a walk round London.
  • Prices change every day. £42 is what this trip cost; check FlixBus for your own dates.
